Suspicion & Insight (DE: Zweifel und Erkenntnis)

Passive Passive Skill Non-Attribute Tech

When using Ecive, enemies in front are inflicted with the Ecive Scan effect.

When attacking enemies inflicted with the Ecive Scan effect, additional damage is dealt.

When an enemy with the Ecive Scan effect dies, the Ecive Scan effect is applied to nearby enemies.

Enemies positioned at the crosshair are guaranteed to receive the Ecive Scan effect; for Colossi, if the crosshair is near a weak point, that weak point receives the Ecive Scan effect.

When decrypting an Encrypted Vault, efficiency increases, and the reward amount increases upon successful decryption.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Breach Projection (DE: Breschenprojektion)

Skill 1 Active Skill Non-Attribute Tech

Fires a barrage of Breach Shards, dealing damage to hit enemies.

If the enemy hit is inflicted with Ecive Scan, an additional effect is triggered.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Breach Link (DE: Breschenring)

Skill 2 Active Skill Non-Attribute Fusion

Creates an Arche Breach at a designated location. Inflicts a debuff on enemies near the created Arche Breach and spawns an Anti-Gravity Field on the ground. The debuff of the Arche Breach intensifies over time and further weakens Adaptive Defense. Reusing the skill retrieves the Arche Breach. On retrieval, the Anti-Gravity Field disappears, dealing Explosion Damage to the area around it and refunding a portion of the Cooldown.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Gravitic Reversal (DE: Antigravitationsphänomen)

Skill 3 Active Skill Non-Attribute Fusion

Detonates an Arche Breach, causing Gravitic Reversal.

Inflicts Tow on enemies near the Arche Breach and deals Explosion Damage to them.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Gaze of the Abyss (DE: Abgrundblick)

Skill 4 Active Skill Non-Attribute Tech

Grants Gaze of the Abyss.

All Firearm Attacks from Nell target Weak Points, increasing the Weak Point Damage of herself and nearby allies.

While Gaze of the Abyss is active, hitting a target inflicted with Ecive Scan with a Breach Shard increases the duration of Gaze of the Abyss.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Story

"Was I really part of their family?
Wasn't I just another Hathaway making a 'rational' choice?"

– Nell, in the official library story

Nell Hathaway was already irreplaceable as a capable Executive Officer of Albion Headquarters and an Operations Controller who oversaw most of the Operations she planned herself. The staff cadets envied her, and her strategic judgment was considered to have surpassed Alpha's.

Her family had "rationally" forced her out to join the academy so they would have one less mouth to feed; when contact with them was lost after they moved to Axion, she felt nothing. She came to see Alpha, the Descendants and the soldiers of Albion as her family – her Operations prioritized survival over success, and many trusted and followed her because of it.

That belief turned to doubt when she had to decide which "family members" would live and which would die: leaving ten to save a hundred, sacrificing fifty to save a thousand. She began to fear she was just another Hathaway making "rational" choices, and the screams of soldiers lost in Operations took away even her prescribed hours of sleep.

When those doubts led to her worst accident, Nell finally faced her true self. Her doubts became an abyss that reached to the ends of her mind and yielded her Arche. She resolved to embrace that part of herself – believing that faith in victory can come from the doubts of defeat – and vowed to believe in her doubts and use them to her advantage.

Summary of the official library story (Story tab).

Exclusive Equipment / Ability: Exclusive Arche, Breach Entropy Control

The Vulgus and humanity share two concepts: dimension – the boundaries dividing the Void, Ingris and other worlds, passed on to humanity through the Magisters – and Breach, damage done to the Dimensional Wall.

They differ in how they use it: the Vulgus artificially create Breaches to tear down the Dimensional Wall, while humanity replaces the broken Dimensional Wall with Arche and uses the replaced Breach entropy as a weapon against the enemy. Nell is still the only human who can use it that way.

"You've always had a lot of doubts.
So, I trust that when given such great power, you'll use it carefully."

– Alpha, handing Nell her appointment as Chief Executive Officer
